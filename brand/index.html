The mark is one frame of work that finishes on time

Script, render and paint run one after another, each on its own row the way a profiler draws them. All three end before the vsync line, with a sliver of headroom left over. That is the promise of framebudget, at 16 pixels.

Grid 32 units Corner radius 8 units Vsync line x = 25

Logo use

The dark lockup is the default: framebudget lives on Night. Use the mark alone where the name is already on screen: favicons, avatars, the npm page, small UI.

Primary. The tile drops away and the bars sit straight on the page, wordmark in Chalk. lockup-dark.svg

Light documentation and print on white. The tile comes back to hold the bars. lockup-light.svg

One color. Bars and line are cut out of the tile, so the staircase still reads. lockup-mono-ink.svg

Single color white on photos, print and brand color fields. lockup-mono-white.svg

Clear space

x

Keep x on every side, where x is a quarter of the mark height (the tile's corner radius). Nothing else enters that space, including other logos.

Minimum size

Below 24 px always use the tile mark: browser tabs and app bars can be any color, and the tile keeps the bars together.

Do and don't

  • Do Use the dark lockup on Night, Ink or any deep color.

  • Do Switch to the light lockup on Grid or white.

  • Do Go single color on busy or colored fields.

  • Do Use the tile mark alone when the name is nearby.

  • Don't Recolor the bars. Amber, violet, mint, always in that order.

  • Don't Rotate or flip. Work flows left to right, top to bottom.

  • Don't Stretch, squash or re-space the lockup.

  • Don't Put the tileless mark on light backgrounds. Amber disappears.

Icons

Drawn from the same parts as the mark: horizontal bars, square frames and one vertical line. They label effects and controls, they never decorate.

  • 24 unit grid, 2 unit padding, 2 unit stroke with round caps and joins.
  • Rectangles before circles, horizontal before vertical, the way a timeline is drawn.
  • One color, inherited from the text. A single Vsync stroke is allowed only when the icon is about the deadline.
  • Ship at 16, 20 or 24 px. Below 16 px use a label instead.

Motion

A library about frame time has to spend its own carefully. Every animation in the brand passes the test it sells.

  • Motion is a cost

    Animate only to show what changed. Nothing moves on its own except the one live demo on a page, and that stops when it scrolls away.

  • Count in frames

    Durations are whole frames at 60 Hz: 6, 10, 16 or 30. Writing them as frames keeps the budget visible.

  • Leave fast, arrive soft

    An effect that no longer fits exits in 10 frames on the drop curve. One that comes back settles in over 16, with no overshoot.

  • Respect the setting

    With reduced motion, every state change still happens, instantly. No autoplay, no transitions, no parallax.

Code

Code sits on Deep, one step below the page, in every theme, so examples look the same in dark pages and light docs. Five token colors, no background highlights, comments in Mist.

Reading a meter

  • The track runs from 0 to 20 ms, so the Vsync line always sits at the same place.
  • Segments are laid end to end in the order the site ranked them.
  • Effects that do not fit leave the track and are listed struck through, with their cost on this device.
  • Effects are admitted up to 14.2 ms, 85% of the frame. The rest is margin for the governor.
  • The hatched zone past the line is time the device does not have.

Voice and taglines

Talk like a good profiler: exact numbers, plain verbs, no hype. Say what the device can afford, never that a device is bad.

  • Keep the effects. Lose the stutter.

    Primary. Short enough for the npm description and the hero.

  • Every effect costs frame time. Spend it where the device can pay.

    Explains the idea in one breath. Good for talks and the README intro.

  • 16.7 ms per frame. framebudget decides what fits.

    For developers who already know the number. Social posts, stickers.

Writing rules

  • framebudget is always lowercase, even at the start of a sentence.
  • Give the number with its unit: 14.2 ms, score 137, under 1 KB.
  • Effects are allowed or stepped down, never banned or killed.
  • Privacy is a fact, not a feature: "Nothing leaves the device."
